# txhome: Explore How Texas Housing Markets Weathered the 2008 Financial Crisis

Provides cleaned monthly housing market data for 46 Texas market areas
from 2000 to 2015, including median prices adjusted for inflation, a
function that summarises how far each market fell during the 2008
financial crisis and how quickly it recovered, and an interactive Shiny
app for comparing the resilience of different markets. Housing data
originally from the TAMU Real Estate Center, as distributed in the
'ggplot2' package; inflation data from the U.S. Bureau of Labor
Statistics, retrieved from FRED.
